Progression FAQ

What are the 4 new guns in the One Shot Autumn Event?

The official event card only says “4 NEW GUNS”; it does not name them. We have not found a reliable list yet, so we do not guess names.

How long does One Shot Ranked last?

The Ranked event is scheduled until Oct 16, 2026 at 17:55 UTC. Whether it stays as a permanent mode afterwards has not been announced.

Are there game passes in One Shot?

No. Roblox's public game-pass list for One Shot was empty on Oct 2, 2026.

A practical approach

  • Redeem the working crate codes first; they are free.
  • Check the current daily quest requirements before choosing a play goal.
  • Treat cosmetic cases as optional; do not assume a particular item or outcome.
  • Use Ranked and the leaderboard as a challenge, not as proof of permanent rank or reward.
