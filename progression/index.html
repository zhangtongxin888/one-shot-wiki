What we do not claim

The official public listing does not provide enough detail for us to publish exact experience requirements, reward tables, case odds, rarity probabilities, leaderboard scoring rules, or reset schedules. Those values may also change as the experience is updated.

VERIFY IN GAME

Use the current in-game interface as the source for live costs, odds, reward values, and timers. Do not spend Robux based only on an unofficial guide.

A practical approach

  • Check the current daily quest requirements before choosing a play goal.
  • Treat cosmetic cases as optional; do not assume a particular item or outcome.
  • Use leaderboard position as a challenge, not as proof of permanent rank or reward.
  • Recheck the official listing and in-game notices after major updates.
