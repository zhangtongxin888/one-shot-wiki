How we write

  • Official fact: directly supported by a creator-controlled public source.
  • Strategy: general advice inferred from the documented game format and clearly presented as guidance.
  • Live value: costs, odds, counts, and balance details are omitted unless a reliable current source is available.
  • Unknown: we say what we cannot verify instead of filling the gap.
