The combat rhythm

Fire. Break line of sight. Reload.

The official game description is simple and unforgiving: every gun holds one bullet. Good play is less about spraying and more about timing your exposure, placing one clean shot, and staying difficult to track while your weapon cycles.

01 / ACQUIRE

Find the clean angle

Hold your crosshair where an opponent is likely to appear. Less correction means a faster, calmer shot.

Aim fundamentals →
02 / COMMIT

Spend the bullet

Fire when the target is readable—not because the weapon is ready. A miss creates a vulnerable reload window.

Learn the match loop →
03 / RESET

Move before reloading

Slide, strafe, and use cover to change the next angle. Reappearing in the same place makes you predictable.
