The combat rhythm

Fire. Break line of sight. Reload.

The official game description is simple and unforgiving: every gun holds one bullet. Good play is less about spraying and more about timing your exposure, placing one clean shot, and staying difficult to track while your weapon cycles.

01 / ACQUIRE

Find the clean angle

Hold your crosshair where an opponent is likely to appear. Less correction means a faster, calmer shot.

Aim fundamentals →
02 / COMMIT

Spend the bullet

Fire when the target is readable—not because the weapon is ready. A miss creates a vulnerable reload window.

Learn the match loop →
03 / RESET

Move before reloading

Slide, strafe, and use cover to change the next angle. Reappearing in the same place makes you predictable.

One Shot Wiki FAQ

Quick answers

Is there an official One Shot wiki or Trello?

No official wiki or Trello for Shooter Game Group's One Shot was found on Oct 2, 2026. The Roblox listing links only the game and its group. The widely shared “One Shot Trello” is for One Shot Remastered by Tempest Media, a different game.

What are the newest One Shot codes?

SUMMER (1 Release Crate) and FREE13 (1 Crate) are listed as working by at least three code trackers. FREE12 (1 Premium Crate) is listed as working by some and expired by others. Join the Shooter Game Group before redeeming in the Store.

When does the One Shot Autumn Event end?

The official Roblox event card runs until Oct 16, 2026 at 18:55 UTC and promises 4 new guns.

Does One Shot have a Ranked mode?

Yes, as an official event: “RANKED – REGISTER NOW!”, scheduled from Oct 2, 2026 18:00 UTC to Oct 16, 2026 17:55 UTC. Rank tiers and rewards were not published when we checked.

Who made One Shot on Roblox?

Shooter Game Group, a verified Roblox community with about 212,000 members. Do not confuse it with One Shot Remastered (Tempest Media) or other “one shot” modes in games like Rivals.
